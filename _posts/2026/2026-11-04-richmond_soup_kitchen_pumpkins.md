Angela helps at the Richmond Soup Kitchen several times a winter.  Since they stopped having volunteers cooking, I now help once a year with some food prep.

This year I headed to St. Matthias in Richmond and did about three hours of cleaning the church's kitchen and cutting up pumpkins. (The image shows about half of the pumpkins.) They were donated from Kew Gardens' kitchen garden and they are all heritage varieties.  We got through about 12 before the next group came. Two chefs were busy making them into soups and pasta sauces.

Always good fun and it feels good.
