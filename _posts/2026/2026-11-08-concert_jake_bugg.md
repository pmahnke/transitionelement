Today we went to a nightclub in Kingston, called Circuits, to see a Banquet Records interview and mini-concert by Jake Bugg.

We first saw Jake in 2013 at Shepard's Bush Empire when he was 16 or 17. Now he is seven albums into his career and still doing well; albeit, not has big as that first big tour. But he was great.

He comes off as a really nice guy who is bright and very self aware.

Songs from his new album, "Alamo" were good and he also mixed in a few hits from his back catalog. It was really fun.
